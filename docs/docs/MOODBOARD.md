# Aurum Drive — Creative Moodboard & Research Syntheses

Research analysis across automotive luxury benchmarks, theme references, and premier digital showcases.

| # | Reference / Benchmark | What We Take | What We Deliberately Do Not Take |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **LimoDrive (ref-01)** | Inset rounded cinematic video frame, gold outlined pill label, halo play button, white monochrome brand logos. | Generic cream background; hotlinked assets; unstyled video embeds. |
| 2 | **Rentgo Footer (ref-02)** | Dark tire-tread chevron texture, soft top radial gold spotlight, balanced 4-column information architecture. | Bright yellow/amber hue (`#F5B754`); template typos ("Phone Numer"); demo seller promotional badges. |
| 3 | **LimoDrive FAQ (ref-03)** | 2-column layout (sticky heading with gold underline + accordion), active gold highlighted card, bottom 3-pillar features. | Non-responsive text; disconnected filler answers in Q5; cream low-contrast styling. |
| 4 | **LimoDrive Pointer (ref-04)** | Interactive 24px gold trailing ring cursor with smooth easing. | Disruptive cursor hijacking on touch devices; lagging canvas overlays that hurt FPS. |
| 5 | **Rentgo Hero (ref-05)** | Rounded framed car hero, glass floating reservation card, prominent dual action buttons with circular arrow badges. | "Buy Now" theme seller banners; static non-functional inquiry form; generic white backgrounds. |
| 6 | **LOVEHA Flow (ref-06)** | Exact 3-step qualification reservation funnel (Coords -> License -> Dispatch), live period breakdown, deposit reminder. | Plain utilitarian aesthetics; lack of real-time eligibility feedback; missing smooth micro-transitions. |
| 7 | **LuxeDrive Vehicle (ref-07)** | Self-drawing car line-art profile, high-density spec chips, degressive pricing breakdown (day/week/month). | Mismatched photos (BMW header with Rolls interior); typos ("Lether"); static non-interactive booking widget. |
| 8 | **Why Choose Us (ref-08)** | Asymmetric overlapping rounded imagery, gold 10K satisfaction badge with avatar stack, 4 clean icon cards. | Low-res generic assets; non-animated counters; static flat cards. |
| 9 | **Rolls-Royce Motor Cars** | Whisper-quiet luxury restraint, architectural typography, black and champagne lacquer tones. | Overly heavy proprietary video loaders that stall mobile performance. |
| 10 | **Porsche Digital Experience** | Precision telemetry dials, exact technical specs grid, tactile toggle states. | Clinical sterile grey palette lacking warm hospitality. |
| 11 | **Aston Martin Configurator** | Dramatic low-key photographic grading, headlight flare accents, subtle motion blur. | Heavy 3D WebGL requirements that exclude lower-end mobile devices. |
| 12 | **Polestar Web UI** | Clean Swiss typography hierarchy, uncluttered breathing room, pure functional clarity. | Monochrome starkness devoid of ceremonial luxury warmth. |
| 13 | **Blacklane Chauffeur** | Effortless airport & corporate booking flow clarity, transparent assurances. | Rigid corporate feel with no automotive emotion. |
| 14 | **Sixt Luxury Fleet** | Instant dynamic date and location query filtering, crisp category categorization. | Aggressive discount banners and flashing promotional popups. |
| 15 | **Godly / Awwwards Automotive** | Physical momentum scroll, smooth mask-reveal headlines, lateral car entries. | "AI slop" neon purple gradients, floating glowing blobs, unconstrained CPU overhead. |
